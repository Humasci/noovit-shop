## URLs: recommendation
- **Rename now, before launch.** The site isn't indexed yet, so new URLs cost nothing in rankings today; after launch every
  change needs redirects and loses some signal.
- **Do:** hubs and tests (`/womens-health`, `/mens-health`, `/at-home-tests/<keyword>-test`) and the product slugs above
  whose names don't say what they are (`magnesium-complex` → `magnesium-glycinate`, `turmeric-complex` →
  `turmeric-glucosamine-chondroitin`, etc.). Old URLs redirect permanently (301).
- **Keep:** URLs that are already descriptive (`creatine-for-women`, `sleep-calm-stack`, guides, Journal posts).
- Keywords in URLs are a small ranking factor; the bigger win is the title, H1 and content matching the primary keyword,
  which the pages already do.
