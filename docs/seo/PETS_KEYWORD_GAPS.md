# Pet keyword gaps, dogs then cats (US, research 19 Nov 2026)

**Method:** Google Trends pulled directly (US, 12 months), every term compared with the anchor "cat uti test". Relative
interest was converted to rough monthly volume with 5 Exploding Topics figures, so **volumes are ±2× estimates
("TR-est")**. "<thr" = below the Trends threshold (low, not zero); "AC" = Google autocomplete suggests it. Page-1 checks
used a web search tool (no Shopping or AI Overview results; Reddit is probably under-shown).
**Confirm the top picks in Search Atlas or Keyword Planner before building.**

**Finding:** the winnable terms are almost all **questions and explainers**, not product head terms. Product terms
belong to Chewy, PetSmart and Walmart; DNA and colour-changing litter terms belong to Embark, Wisdom Panel, PrettyLitter
and Purina.

## Claims (FDA CVM)
- **Supplements:**
  - DSHEA (the human supplement law) doesn't apply to pets. FDA tolerates structure/function wording plus NASC standards.
  - **Allowed:** "supports normal joint function / mobility / digestive health / urinary tract health / calm behavior / healthy aging".
  - **Never:** treats, cures, prevents, arthritis, anxiety, UTI, crystals, kidney disease, "acidifies urine", "extends lifespan".
- **Test kits:**
  - No FDA pre-approval needed, but labels can't be false or misleading, and FTC requires evidence.
  - Say "screens / monitors", "outside range → see your vet". Mention false negatives.
