# Human keyword gaps: products and test kits (US, research 19 Nov 2026)

**Method:** Google Trends (US, 12 months), every term measured against "creatine" (set to Treendly's 368K/mo). Each
volume is shown as a range of that figure to 4×; rankings within a table are reliable (±20%), but absolute numbers may be
off by 2–4×. Competition comes from ~35 page-1 checks with a web-search tool (not Google). **Confirm the top 20 in
Search Atlas.**

## Findings
1. **At-home test searches are not a gap for a new site.** Superpower.com has built guides for "ferritin test at home",
   "B12 test kit" and "vitamin D test kit", and Healthline, mindbodygreen, testing.com, LetsGetChecked, Everlywell and Labcorp
   cover the rest. Most "X test at home" terms are small (50–340/mo).
2. **Our sleep/stress range has the weakest competition.** Page 1 for magnesium and ashwagandha long-tails is
   Cymbiotika's mass-produced pages, thin Indian health sites, Huberman AI pages and ubiehealth: beatable.
3. Disease-adjacent terms (lion's mane for ADHD, CoQ10 + statins, magnesium for anxiety) are small or held by medical
   sites, and carry claim risk. Skip or reframe.
