# Keyword plan by rank priority (20 Nov 2026)

Source: owner's Ahrefs Keywords Explorer (US) screenshots, 20 Nov, 72 keywords (`ahrefs-2026-11-20-raw.md`).
KD = keyword difficulty (0–100), SV = monthly US searches, TP = traffic potential (traffic the #1 page gets across all its
keywords). **Nothing can rank while the site is noindex**: "act now" means the page is ready and optimized on launch day,
then submitted in Search Console with the sitemap.

## How the tiers work
| Tier | Rule | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **P1: act now** | KD 0–10 | A new site can rank with one good page and a few links. Build/optimize before launch. |
| **P2: next** | KD 11–25 | Winnable in 2–6 months with a strong page plus internal links and some backlinks. Pages exist now; strengthen after launch. |
| **P3: build up** | KD 26–50 | Needs topical authority: several linked articles and real backlinks. Target from month 3. |
| **P4: long game** | KD 51+ | Don't chase directly yet. Covered by supporting content; revisit when the domain has authority. |
