# New product categories (not supplements): white-label scan (research, 19 Nov 2026)

Web research only; no supplier contacted, nothing signed up for. Minimums and prices come from vendor pages or search
snippets and are **unconfirmed until a written quote**. OK Capsule's pricing page (401) and Muhdo's page could not be
opened; their figures come from snippets. Companion reports: `LAB_TESTS_WHITE_LABEL.md` (blood tests),
`NEW_PRODUCT_LINES.md` (supplement additions), sleep/snoring devices (separate report).

**Bottom line:** four categories are worth a first step now: **nano-hydroxyapatite toothpaste**, a **home sleep apnea test
referral** (Lofta-type), a **small personalised-pack test** (OK Capsule) and **paid digital programs** built from our own
guides. White-label blood tests (Spot/SiPhox) sit just below these. Most "trend" tests (microbiome, DNA, epigenetic age,
IgG food sensitivity) fail our evidence bar.

## Candidates

### 1. Nano-hydroxyapatite toothpaste: now
- Fluoride-free, enamel-remineralising paste. A daily repeat purchase that fits "evidence-led".
- **Growth:** ~$422M (2025) → ~$501M (2026) → $1.69B by 2033, ~19%/yr ([GVR](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/nano-hydroxyapatite-toothpaste-market-report)). Boka reported ~481% retail growth in 2025 and is rolling into 4,500 Walmart stores ([Glossy](https://www.glossy.co/?p=206360), [Happi](https://www.happi.com/?p=261243)). Busy (Boka, RiseWell, Amazon sellers) but no giant.
- **Partners:** Metro Private Label, 500-unit startup minimum ([Metro](https://www.metroprivatelabel.com/private-label-toothpaste/)). Cinoll: ready 10% nHA formula, ~30 days brief to sample ([Cinoll](https://www.cinoll.com/tech-lab/)). Alibaba lists 10–72 pieces, but tube minimums are higher ([Alibaba guide](https://seller.alibaba.com/blogs/2026/southeast-asia/oral-care/toothpaste-product-configuration-guide-alibaba-b2b)). **No dropship found:** we'd hold stock at a 3PL.
- **Rules:** sell as a cosmetic, **no anticavity claim**. FDA called HAP pastes with such claims "unapproved new drugs" (Dr. Brite, Nov 2024, [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/dr-brite-llc-684466-11212024)). ADA acceptance needs fluoride ([ADA](https://www.ada.org/resources/ada-library/oral-health-topics/toothpastes)). MoCRA facility registration and product listing (confirm with FDA).
- **Evidence:** adults 18 months, n=189, HAP non-inferior to 1450 ppm fluoride ([PMC10393266](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10393266/)). Children: non-inferior ([PMC7846568](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7846568/)). Review: 4 RCTs only ([PMC8641555](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8641555)). Trials mostly used micro-HAP; a nano-specific trial was not found.

### 2. Home sleep apnea test referral: now
- A partner such as Lofta: its independent doctors prescribe the WatchPAT One test, read it and diagnose. $189 (promotions $99–139) ([Lofta](https://lofta.com/pages/need-a-prescription), [how it works](https://lofta.com/pages/how-it-works)).
- **Partner model:** co-branded pages for other companies, e.g. [Blain's](https://lofta.com/pages/blains-partnership-hst) ($139) and [SomnoMed](https://lofta.com/products/somnomed-referral-home-sleep-test). Commission: not found.
- **Rules:** prescription device, so the partner carries the medical side. We make no diagnostic claims, disclose paid referrals (FTC) and hold no health data.
- **Evidence:** strong. AASM accepts home testing for uncomplicated adults with suspected moderate–severe OSA (Kapur 2017, [JCSM](https://jcsm.aasm.org/doi/10.5664/jcsm.6506), cited from memory, not reopened). NCOA warns against tests sold without a prescription ([NCOA](https://ncoa.org/adviser/sleep/home-sleep-apnea-tests)).
- A natural next step for the Sleep line and the snoring research.

### 3. Personalised daily packs: small test
- **OK Capsule:** 30-day personalised packs, Shopify app or API, "no minimums, no inventory risk" ([pricing](https://okcapsule.squarespace.com/pricing), [solutions](https://okcapsule.squarespace.com/solutions)). API page: packaging from $21/unit plus supplements per pack plus shipping from $7.50 (may be out of date) ([API page](https://okcapsule.squarespace.com/api-instructions)). Shopify app $100/mo ([Shopify](https://apps.shopify.com/ok-capsule-pack-builder)).
- **Warning:** Bayer closed Care/of in June 2024 ([NutraIngredients](https://www.nutraingredients-usa.com/Article/2024/06/28/The-rise-and-fall-of-Care-of-A-financial-perspective), [Retail Dive](https://www.retaildive.com/news/careof-is-shutting-down/719106)). Vitl charges £39.95 for 28 days ([Vitl](https://intercom.help/vitl/en/articles/11790781-how-much-is-a-personalised-subscription)).
- **Margin looks thin:** ~$28.50+ per pack before ingredients. Test three fixed packs (Sleep, Calm, Focus) only if the margin holds. See also `docs/DAILY_PACK_CONCEPT.md`.

### 4. Paid digital programs: now
- A 4–8-week sleep, stress or focus program built from our guides, quiz and advisor. On our own Next.js site it costs almost nothing. App shells: WellCore $40/mo ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10048192/WellCore-Solutions/)) to Trainerize $204/mo ([FitBudd](https://www.fitbudd.com/insights/white-label-fitness-app-pricing-what-you-actually-pay?c84a98fb_page=6)).
- **Rules:**
  - FDA's 2026 general wellness guidance relaxed the rules for low-risk software ([Faegre Drinker](https://faegredrinker.com/en/insights/publications/2026/1/key-updates-in-fdas-2026-general-wellness-and-clinical-decision-support-software-guidance)).
  - Don't claim to treat insomnia: digital CBT-I is an FDA-cleared category (e.g. [SleepioRx](https://www.bighealth.com/sleepio-rx)).
  - FTC Health Breach Notification Rule ([Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2024/05/30/2024-10855/health-breach-notification-rule)).
  - Washington My Health My Data Act, which lets consumers sue ([Kelley Drye](https://www.kelleydrye.com/viewpoints/blogs/ad-law-access/my-health-my-data-washingtons-health-data-privacy-revolution)).
- **Evidence:** digital CBT-I works (NICE more cautious, [NICE](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/HTG624/chapter/3-evidence)). A habit program "based on" it is weaker.

### 7. Red light therapy: later
- $533.8M (2025), ~9.8%/yr ([GVR](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/red-light-therapy-market-report)).
- OEM panels $125–159, 200 MOQ for a custom logo ([Alibaba](https://www.alibaba.co.uk/product-detail/Oem-Odm-Desktop-Red-Light-Panel_1601681243845.html)).

### 8. OTC hearing aids: later / skip
- FDA OTC rule 2022 ([Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2022/08/17/2022-17230/medical-devices-ear-nose-and-throat-devices-establishing-over-the-counter-hearing-aids)). OTC adds only 5.7 points to adoption ([BHI](https://www.betterhearing.org/newsroom/blogs/tis-the-season-for-giving-open-access-to-marketrak-2025-insights/)). Sony exited ([Consumer Reports](https://www.consumerreports.org/health/hearing-aids/over-the-counter-hearing-aids-brands-comparison-a3246025494/)).
- Partners: Jinghao OEM, 200 MOQ ([HKTDC](https://sourcing.hktdc.com/en/Product-Detail/JINGHAO-A26D-Invisible-In-Ear-OTC-Hearing-Aids-Rechargeable-2025-1Z03OMDT8)); [Audio Service](https://audioservice.com/en/service/white-label).
- Evidence good ([PMC10102918](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10102918/), [JSLHR](https://pubs.asha.org/doi/10.1044/2024_JSLHR-24-00423)), but heavy support and returns.

### 9–17. Skips and parks (short)
- **Cortisol/hormone saliva:** practitioner channels only ([Fullscript](https://us.fullscript.com/labs/zrt-laboratory/saliva-cortisol)); NY/NJ/RI limits. Fold into the Junction evaluation.
- **OTC CGM:** Stelo $99 for 2 sensors ([MedTech Dive](https://www.medtechdive.com/news/dexcom-sells-stelo-over-the-counter-cgm/725310/)), Lingo $49+ ([Fierce](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/medtech/over-counter-cgm-market-grows-abbotts-lingo-debut)). No white-label. No benefit without prediabetes ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41588451/), [GU](https://www.gu.se/en/news/no-evidence-that-continuous-glucose-monitors-improve-health-in-people-without-diabetes)).
- **Epigenetic age:** reliability doubts ([Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s43682-026-00044-8), [Stanford](https://news.stanford.edu/stories/2026/09/biological-age-tests-tips-advice)).
- **Microbiome:** [Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/at-home-microbiome-tests-reveal-dramatically-different-results/) (same sample, very different results).
- **DNA/nutrigenomics:** genetic part unproven ([PMC9570623](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9570623)); privacy risk.
- **Smart rings:** Oura 74% share; patent risk ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/wearables/2026/smart-ring-shipments-projected-to-jump-49percent/)).
- **Functional drinks:** high co-packer minimums ([Assemblies](https://www.assemblies.com/contract-packaging-moqs/)).
- **GLP-1 telehealth:** FDA warning-letter waves 2025–26 ([PharmTech](https://www.pharmtech.com/view/why-the-fda-issued-30-warning-letters-to-telehealth-sites-marketing-compounded-glp-1s)).
- **IgG food tests:** not valid ([AAAAI](https://www.aaaai.org/tools-for-the-public/conditions-library/allergies/igg-food-test)).

## Top 4: first step for each
1. **nHA toothpaste.** The owner asks Metro Private Label and Cinoll for written quotes: 10% nHA, fluoride-free, 500 units, tube minimums, stability/COA, MoCRA listing help, shipping to a US 3PL. Claude checks the margin (≥ ~$15 per first order) and drafts cosmetic-only copy.
2. **Sleep apnea test referral.** The owner sends Lofta's partner team a short inquiry (commission, co-branded page), using the Blain's and SomnoMed pages as the model. Claude adds a "Do I need a sleep apnea test?" section to the sleep guide (needs the medical reviewer).
3. **Personalised packs.** Price three fixed packs in OK Capsule's calculator. Get written confirmation of no minimums, current packaging and shipping costs, and API dropship. Go only if the margin holds.
4. **Digital program.** Claude scopes a 4-week "Sleep Reset" by email or account, built from existing guides, quiz and advisor, in general wellness language. Privacy check (FTC breach rule, Washington law) before collecting answers.
