# IHD Lab: at-home tests for Noovit (review + draft enquiry, 19 Nov 2026)

Source: https://www.ihdlab.com/at-home-test-list, /white-label-test-kits, /digital-health-partners (read 19 Nov). No contact made.

## Verdict: worth an enquiry (alongside Spot)
**Good fit**
- Own CLIA lab (Irvine, CA), FDA-registered, CA/NY/MD licences. Kits ship to all 50 states.
- Fully white-labelled kits: our box, instruction cards and requisitions. Their 3PL builds, stores and ships kits overnight, with return shipping included.
- Orders by API (LabFlow) or partner portal, so it can hook into our Stripe checkout.
- Results in 24–72 h. No minimum stated; the smallest volume tier is "under 250 kits a month".
- The menu (68 at-home tests) covers everything our range needs:

| Our goal | Tests on their menu | Collection |
|---|---|---|
| Nutrient check (core panel) | Vitamin D 25-OH, ferritin, vitamin B12, folate, magnesium | Microtainer (finger-prick tube) |
| Energy / heart | hs-CRP, HbA1c, lipids, ApoB, Lp(a), homocysteine | DBS card or Microtainer |
| Thyroid | TSH, free T4, free T3 | DBS or Microtainer |
| Men's / women's hormones | Total and free testosterone, estradiol, SHBG, DHEA-S, cortisol | Mixed |

**Watch-outs**
- **Microtainer** (filling a small tube from a finger-prick) is harder than a blood-spot card, so expect more failed samples and retests. The core nutrient panel is Microtainer-only.
- **Magnesium type not stated** (serum vs RBC). Label it honestly as "serum magnesium".
- **Omega-3 index isn't on the at-home menu.** Add OmegaQuant as an affiliate.
- **Physician ordering isn't explained** on these pages; they have a telehealth-partner page. We must have their (or a partner's) doctor network order every kit.
- **No prices published.**
- **Our side:** HIPAA/BAA, FTC Health Breach Notification Rule (no tracking pixels on results pages), and FTC claims (a low result is a reason to see a clinician, never proof someone needs a Noovit product).

## Suggested first product
**"Nutrient Check"**: vitamin D, ferritin, B12, magnesium (+ folate) in one finger-prick kit. Retail target $79–89 (or $69 with a subscription), if their price allows ≥ ~$30 per kit after costs. Add-on later: "Heart & Energy" (hs-CRP, HbA1c, lipids/ApoB).
