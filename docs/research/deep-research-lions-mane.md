## The headline: our current spec is weak
- **Dose:** positive human trials used **1,800–3,200 mg/day of fruiting-body powder** (Mori 2009: 3,000 mg; Saitsu
  2019: 3,200 mg; Docherty 2023: 1,800 mg), or 1,050 mg/day of an *erinacine A-enriched mycelium* (Li 2020). Supliful's
  product is **1,000 mg/day of crude fruiting body + mycelium powder**: about a third of the fruiting-body trial doses,
  and not the standardized mycelium Li used. A 10 g/day trial in students (Grozier 2022) found no effect, so "more"
  isn't automatically better either.
- **"40% polysaccharides":** a colorimetric total-carbohydrate number that counts grain starch. If the mycelium is grown
  on grain, it can read 40%+ with little fungal material. Only a Megazyme (AOAC 2016.03) **beta-glucan** result, with
  low alpha-glucan (<5%), shows quality.
- **Labeling (lawyer question):** the report says mycelium grown on grain and sold with its substrate should be named as
  such on the label (e.g. "mycelial biomass cultured on organic oats"). Unverified here; ask the lawyer.
- **Price:** at $35 (≈$1.17/day) we'd sell an underdosed crude powder against Real Mushrooms' fruiting-body extract
  (~$0.58/day, published beta-glucan) and Nootropics Depot (~$0.33–0.50/day).

## Do not use (unverified or conflicting)
- Exact Mori 2009 HDS-R means and Li 2020 CASI/MMSE effects as "significant": our verified guide found Mori reports
  figures (we used improver counts) and Li's MMSE difference vs placebo was **not** significant (only daily-living
  scores differed). Treat the report's precise numbers as unverified.
- FDA warning-letter numbers and dates, the "compliance policy guide" on substrates, Health Canada claim wording,
  adverse-event percentages (3–9%), and the r/LionsManeRecovery member count: unchecked.
- Mechanism claims (5-alpha-reductase, kappa-opioid) — speculative.

## Usable
- Honest framing: modest, reversible effects in older adults with mild cognitive impairment at ~3 g/day; little evidence
  in healthy young adults; mood/anxiety evidence small and preliminary; nerve repair is animal-only.
- Gains faded within 4 weeks of stopping in Mori 2009 → continuous use matters; no evidence that cycling is needed.
- Safety: generally well tolerated; rare serious allergic reactions (Nakatsugawa 2003 lung case); avoid with mushroom
  allergy; theoretical caution with blood thinners and diabetes drugs (lab/animal data).
- Claims to avoid entirely: dementia/Alzheimer's, nerve repair/remyelination, depression/anxiety treatment, gastritis.
- PAA ideas for the guide: fruiting body vs mycelium, polysaccharides vs beta-glucans, vivid dreams/insomnia, coffee
  stacks, the "Stamets stack" (no trials).

## Decision (recommended)
Keep Lion's Mane **off the launch** (it is hidden already) until we have either:
1. a **fruiting-body extract** with a Megazyme beta-glucan COA (e.g. hot-water extract ≥25% beta-glucan, or a
   standardized dual extract) from Supliful or another supplier, or
2. Supliful's COA showing high beta-glucan / low alpha-glucan **and** a dose we can defend (e.g. 3 capsules = 1,500 mg,
   still below the trials — honest copy needed).
Don't add "vs placebo" bars to the product page: the trials used ~3× our dose and that would mislead.
