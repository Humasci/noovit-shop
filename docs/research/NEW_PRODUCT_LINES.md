## Rejected
- **Creatine gummies**: huge searches but lab tests (SuppCo, NOW, Eurofins) found many under-dosed; lawsuits; no Supliful option.
- **Mouth tape**: 2025 PLOS One review found little benefit and a suffocation risk; searches −29%, peaked.
- **Nasal strips**: legal (Class I exempt, 21 CFR 874.3900) but off-brand and Breathe Right dominates.
- **Urolithin A**: patent holder (Amazentis/Timeline) sues sellers.
- **Shilajit**: lead/thallium contamination findings; searches −45%.
- **Mushroom coffee**: peaked (−15%), consolidating.
- **Beetroot (Supliful)**: nitrate content not stated, so no performance claim.
- **Inositol**: demand is PCOS (disease framing).
- Taurine, posture sensors, cold therapy/sauna, kids/teens, protein snacks: brand fit, returns, liability or no dropship route.

## Key risks and copy notes
- Creatine: "supports memory"* only; 18+; kidney/pregnancy "consult your doctor"; sodium note on the hydration powder.
- Psyllium: mandatory choking warning for dry psyllium (21 CFR 101.17(f)), full glass of water, space from medicines;
  stay with structure/function ("supports regularity"*) rather than the strict-wording cholesterol health claim.
- Saffron: "supports a positive mood"* only; pregnancy, antidepressant and blood-thinner warnings; require ID test/COA
  (adulteration is common).
- Iron: mandatory overdose warning (21 CFR 101.17(e)), child-resistant packaging, hemochromatosis warning, "test first".
- GLP-1 bundle: never imply it raises GLP-1 or replaces medication; keep "GLP-1" out of product names.
