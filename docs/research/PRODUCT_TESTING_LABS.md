# Third-party product testing labs (research, 19 Nov 2026)

Web research only; no lab contacted. Prices are from published lists unless marked as an anecdote. Amazon's own policy
page didn't render, so its rules come from approved verifiers' guides (Certified Labs, Epstein Becker Green).

## Key finding
**Testing alone does not unlock Amazon or TikTok Shop.** Both also need the **factory's current third-party cGMP
certificate**, which Supliful won't provide.
- **Amazon:** reports go to an approved verifier together with the GMP certificate.
- **TikTok Shop:** the lab report must match the invoice and manufacturer, and cGMP proof is required.

Order of work:
1. Test for our own site ("third-party tested", COAs published).
2. Marketplaces stay blocked until GMP proof exists, or we move to a supplier that provides it.

**Update (19 Nov, owner):** Supliful products are sold on Amazon, and Supliful runs an FBA service (https://help.supliful.com/en/collections/16302765-amazon-fba; Pro annual/Enterprise plans). Its help pages don't name the GMP/COA documents it supplies for verification, so ask Supliful before starting.

## Platform rules
**Amazon (expanded early 2026)**
- Covers all supplements.
- Approved verifiers (Jan 2026; the list changes in Seller Central): Certified Laboratories, Eurofins, Intertek, Mérieux, NSF, SGS, UL.
- Tests can be run at any ISO 17025 lab, but a verifier reviews them. Reports from other labs must be under 6 months old.
- Required: heavy metals, micro, label claim (21 CFR 101.36), pesticides for botanicals. Also an adulterant screen (NSF/ANSI 173) for sports, weight, sexual and joint products. Certified Labs adds sleep and nootropic products.
- Annual, per ASIN (each listing).

**TikTok Shop US**
- Supplements are invite-only.
- Reports must be under 180 days old and match the invoice and seller name/address.
- Required: micro, metals, contaminants and potency.
- Resellers must use a TikTok partner lab: Eurofins, NSF, SGS, UL, TÜV SÜD, Mérieux, Intertek, Certified Laboratories, QIMA, Bureau Veritas, ACT, SGT, API.

## "Organic" and KSM-66 (for the record; the owner decided on 19 Nov to keep both, as Supliful lists them)
- **Organic** ([NOP PM 11-7](https://www.ams.usda.gov/sites/default/files/media/NOP-PM-11-7-PrivateLabelCertification.pdf), [7 CFR 205.101](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/7/205.101), [Oregon Tilth](https://tilth.org/knowledgebase_category/brand-owners-marketers-and-brokers)):
  - A private-label brand doesn't need its own certification.
  - But the certified handler must be named on the label ("Certified organic by …"), and the certifier is needed for that line.
  - A lab test can't prove organic status.
- **KSM-66** is a registered trademark of Ixoreal ([Reg. 4551163](https://trademark.justia.com/861/00/ksm-66-86100339.html)). Licensed brands state the licence. Truthful use of a genuine ingredient's name is often defended as nominative fair use (not legal advice). A lab can confirm ashwagandha root, not the brand.
- **Low-cost way to back both:** ask Supliful only for the organic certifier's name and its KSM-66 supplier statement. Otherwise, have the regulatory lawyer (brief 02) confirm.
- **Prop 65 safe harbors** (from memory, verify with OEHHA): lead 0.5 µg/day, cadmium 4.1, methylmercury 0.3, inorganic arsenic 10.
