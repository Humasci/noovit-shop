## Angles and look
- **"KSM-66" in the title and on the label** is the main signal (Revive, Physician's Choice, Tribe, Nootropics Depot, Goli). If we can use the name, we should.
- **Big numbers:** Physician's Choice shows "1,000 mg" and "15 mg black pepper" in circles on the label.
- **"With black pepper"** appears often (Brainstorm, Fernwood, Physician's Choice). Absorption marketing again.
- **Look:** mostly white bottles with coloured labels. Premium cues: Thorne minimal white, SuperFeast black glass, Tribe maroon jar, InfiniWell clear glass in a lifestyle photo.

## What it means for Noovit
1. **Don't sell Supliful's Ashwagandha Plus as is at a premium.** It's the identical product to three Vitasy brands at $29.99. If we use it, price at **$30–32 one-time / $27 subscribed** and win on content and the evidence story.
2. **Better: a plain KSM-66 600 mg** (ask Supliful; otherwise another supplier). It's cleaner for "Calm+", it isn't the Vitasy product, and "KSM-66, 600 mg, the dose used in the studies" is a strong, honest label line.
3. **Bundles carry the margin.** Ashwagandha is in the **Sleep & Calm Stack**; 3- and 6-packs earn 74–77%.
4. **Margin is thinner here:** about $17.80 (59%) at $30 on an **$11 placeholder cost**. Send the real price.
5. **Content opportunity:** a guide on ashwagandha (KSM-66 vs Sensoril vs root powder, the doses used in studies, thyroid and liver cautions, and why some countries restrict it) is exactly our evidence-graded angle, and most results are just shops.
