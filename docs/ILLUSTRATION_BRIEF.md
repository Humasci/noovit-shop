# Illustration brief: Noovit product labels (3 Nov 2026)

**What we need:** six original illustrations (one per product), drawn in a "made of fine particles" style, to sit in a
fixed square block in the top-right corner of every label. Later: the same treatment for about 10 more products.

## The label (fixed; please don't redesign)
- White HDPE bottle, white cap (Supliful print-on-demand). Label 6.0" × 2.25" wrap; front panel 2.2" × 2.25".
- Front: small "noovit" wordmark top-left, category and dose under it, product name below, product-colour band along the
  bottom. The illustration sits in a **square-ish block top-right**, about 1.2" × 1.1", that **bleeds off the top and
  right edges** and has a soft, ragged left and bottom edge with a few stray particles (see the drafts).
- Current drafts (code-generated, to show placement, scale and colour only):
  `design/label-v2-2026-11/closeup-F2-illustrations-1.png`, `closeup-F2-crystals.png`, `family-F2-final-draft.png`.

## Style
- Built from **fine round particles** (about 0.2–0.3 mm at print size), shaded so the subject has volume (light from the
  upper left), over a pale tinted particle ground that fills the block. Think scientific molecular illustration / 3D
  particle renders, but calm and editorial, not glossy stock art.
- **One colour family per product**, from light to deep, plus at most one small orange accent (#FF5F00, the logo cross).
- Premium, precise, quiet. No outlines, no text, no gradients behind the block.

## Deliverables
- Per product: **vector** (SVG or AI/PDF) at the block size, plus a 300 dpi RGB PNG with transparency.
- Must print cleanly on Supliful's digital label printing: no particle smaller than ~0.15 mm, no hairline detail.
- Full ownership / exclusive commercial licence for packaging, web, ads and social. Original work only: no stock,
  no AI images trained on others' illustrations without clear rights, no copying of competitors' artwork.
- Optional: larger web versions (homepage hero, product pages, social), and a looping animation of particles assembling.

- Quotes for the set of six; budget roughly $300–800. First sketches of 2 products, then the rest.
