## 2. Angles
### A. Price tiers (per bottle)
- **Under $15:** 7 listings. Big brands, iHerb and resellers. You can't win here on margin: Supliful costs $8–11.
- **$15–27:** 6 listings. Organic, liquid, chews and bulk.
- **$29–40 (premium DTC):** 7 listings. **Momentous, Paleovalley, BetterVits, Thorne, Absorption Co., NAOMI, Future Kind.** This is **our tier**.
- **$45+:** Gaia, Nature's Sunshine, big packs and bundles.

### B. Price per day
Premium brands sit at **$1.00–1.33/day**; value brands at $0.12–0.50. Our $1.33 one-time is **the most expensive** in the premium tier. Subscribed ($1.17) and the 6-pack ($1.00) are in line with it.

### C. The claim each brand leads with
| Angle | Who | Note for us |
|---|---|---|
| **Absorption multiplier** ("149×", "45.5×", phytosome) | Absorption Co., Life Extension, Thorne | The most-used premium angle. Only valid with a patented or studied form; generic black pepper can't claim a multiplier |
| **Organic / whole food** | Paleovalley, NatureWise, Future Kind, Global Healing | Needs organic certification |
| **Potency number** (1000/1200/2000 mg) | NAOMI, Nutrition Geeks, Nature's Sunshine, Bulk | Big numbers grab attention; mg of what (root vs curcuminoids) is often unclear |
| **Clinical / trust seals** (USP, pharmacist, clinicians) | Nature Made, Thorne, NAOMI | Strongest premium trust lever |
| **Format** (chews, liquid, pouch) | Humann, Global Healing, Nutrition Geeks | A way to stand out in the grid |
| **Discount / first-order price** | Momentous, Absorption Co., ProHealth, Nutrition Geeks | Strike-through badges draw the eye |
| **Bundle** | Mara Labs | A rare angle in this grid, so a gap for us |

### D. Trust signals
- **Review count is the moat.** Mass brands have 1k–9k reviews. Premium DTC brands have far fewer (Momentous 136, NAOMI 48, Future Kind 67) yet still rank, thanks to bids, images and brand.
- Some premium brands show **no stars at all** (Absorption Co., BetterVits). Launching without reviews is normal.
- BetterVits shows a **store rating of 3.9/5**, which is a visible negative. Store and seller ratings matter too, via Google Customer Reviews.

### E. Who is selling
- Many listings are **resellers or marketplaces** (iHerb, GNMart, ibspot). They compete on price and fast delivery.
- DTC brands sell on their own sites, which is our model.

### F. Promotions and shipping
- **Sale or strike-through:** 5 of 26 listings. "1st order price" (Momentous) is a subscription-discount angle we could use, with a real price on the product page.
- **Free gift over $75** (Global Healing) is an order-value play.
- **Free delivery dates** appear on most. Ours ships in 3–6 days, which is fine; a delivery date in the feed (shipping settings) helps.
- **Returns:** "90-day returns" shows on NAOMI and Humann. We offer a **90-day guarantee**, so put it in the Merchant Center return policy so it displays.

### G. Look at thumbnail size
Two clear clusters:
1. **Loud and busy** (mass): bright colours, many words and seals.
2. **Quiet and premium** (DTC): white or black bottles, minimal type, one accent (Momentous, Thorne, Absorption Co., Future Kind amber, NAOMI black and gold).

What reads at about 160 px: **bottle colour or silhouette, product name, one number**.

Our current render (white bottle, orange band, small text) sits **between** the two clusters. It's clean, but the name is small and the band is busy (mosaic, several lines of text).

### H. Titles
- Winners front-load **ingredient + key spec**, e.g. "Curcumin Capsules | 149× Higher…" or "Turmeric Tablets 2000…".
- Brand names at the front only help known brands (Thorne, NOW).
- Several titles contain risky claims ("#1 Vegan Inflammation Supplement").
- **Note:** the search was "tablets" but almost every result is a **capsule**. Google matches intent loosely, so we don't need a tablet SKU.

## 3. What this means for Noovit
1. **Compete in the $29–40 premium tier**, never on price against the $6–17 mass tier.
2. **Price:** turmeric at **$36 one-time / $30 subscribed** puts us at Thorne/Absorption level per bottle, with a better subscription price. Alternatively keep $40 and show a real "Subscribe: $35" or a "1st order" price.
3. **Our angle:** "**evidence-graded**". It's honest where competitors overclaim. In the title and on the label: the dose, the standardisation (95% curcuminoids) and the form (with black pepper).
4. **Better turmeric formula option:** a **branded, studied form** (e.g. a phytosome or a water-dispersible curcumin, if Supliful or another supplier has one) is what justifies premium pricing in this category. Plain turmeric with black pepper is the commodity version.
5. **Lead with the bundle:** our **Joint Support Trio** is rare in this grid, so advertise it as a distinct product.
6. **Look:** go fully into the quiet premium cluster (minimal label, bigger name, one accent, maybe amber). Mock-ups are next.
7. **Feed hygiene:**
   - titles like `Noovit Turmeric Complex – 95% Curcuminoids + Black Pepper – 60 Capsules`
   - return policy (90 days) and shipping speed in Merchant Center
   - `sale_price` only for real promotions
8. **Reviews:** we start without stars, like Absorption Co. and BetterVits. Get the first 50 via review emails plus an aggregator, then stars switch on.
