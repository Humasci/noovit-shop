# Google Shopping analysis: "NAD+" (US, 28 Oct 2026)

_Source: owner's 5 screenshots of the Sponsored Products carousel for "nad +" (Denver, CO), 13 listings, plus the Supliful product page. Full table, tiers and profit calculator: `docs/pricing/noovit-pricing.xlsx` (NAD+ tabs)._

## The short version
- **"Mostly liquid" = mostly injectable vials.** 7 of 13 listings are small glass vials of freeze-dried NAD+ powder, sold **"for research use only"**. They're meant to be mixed with water and injected, and 2 more are nasal sprays. That's the peptide grey market (the same sellers as the "research" methylene blue: PeakForce, Mile High). **They aren't supplements and never our lane.** The real oral-supplement market is only **4 listings**.
- **Cortex (Vitasy) almost certainly sells the same Supliful capsule** at **$39.99 with free shipping**. Its label claims ("aids in normal aging process, promotes normal cell function, supports cardiovascular health") mirror Supliful's page. Cortex has shown up in *every* search so far (berberine, lion's mane, methylene blue, NAD+), and its range matches Supliful's catalogue. **Vitasy looks like a large Supliful reseller with several sub-brands (Cortex, Compass).**
- **The evidence brand is Elysium Basis** ($65, 4.3★, 1k+ reviews). It uses **NR** (nicotinamide riboside) plus pterostilbene, not NAD+, and has human trials behind it.

## What it means for Noovit
1. **Selling the same white-label capsule as Cortex is the core problem**, not price. If we launch Supliful NAD+, a shopper can find the same formula for $39.99. We'd have to win on:
   - **honesty:** say clearly that oral NAD+ has limited human evidence, and what the quercetin and resveratrol add
   - **content:** a guide comparing NAD+, NR and NMN and explaining why injections and "research" vials are a different, unregulated world
   - **brand and bundles:** for example a "Healthy ageing" stack
2. **Better product, if we can source it: NR.** It's what the evidence brand (Elysium) and Tru Niagen sell. NR has human trials showing it raises blood NAD+; oral NAD+ mostly doesn't. Ask Supliful (and On Demand Fulfillment, Vitalabs, Makers) for an NR or Niagen option.
3. **If we do launch Supliful NAD+:** price around **$40 one-time / $36 subscribed** (matching Cortex; Elysium sets the ceiling at $65). The margin is about $26 (66%) at $40 on a **$12 placeholder cost**; send the real price.
4. **Look:** the oral premium cues are amber glass with a white minimal label (Innerbody Labs, the brand you liked) and a minimal white jar (Elysium). The vials look clinical, so a clean label B or D would read as "supplement", not "research chemical", which is what we want.

## Claims and safety (if launched)
- Use "supports cellular energy" and "supports healthy aging" with care. Don't say "DNA repair", "longevity", "reverses aging" or compare to IV NAD+.
- Warnings: resveratrol and quercetin can interact with blood thinners and some drug metabolism. Talk to your doctor if you take medication; not for pregnancy or breastfeeding.
- Ask Supliful for a COA showing NAD+ potency over shelf life (NAD+ breaks down with heat and moisture).
