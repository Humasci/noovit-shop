_14 Nov 2026. Goal: real photos of the printed bottles to replace the mockups on the shop, Google Shopping and social._

## Look
Premium, minimal, warm: think Thorne. Dark matte labels on white bottles; warm cream/sand backdrop; soft light from the
front-left; true colours. Our current mockups (`design/label-v3-2026-11/bottles-E2dark-*.png`) show the look and
framing we want, so share them as the reference.

## Before the shoot: print check (send us within a day of delivery)
Supliful prints our labels on demand, and nobody on our side will see a bottle in person. On arrival, send phone photos of
each product (front, both sides, top) and note anything off: colours that look wrong, text too small to read, smudging,
labels crooked or peeling, damaged packaging, the parcel's packing slip (does it show Supliful's name?).

## Shot list (7 products: 5 bottles + 2 powder tubs)
1. Each product, front, on **pure white** (#FFFFFF), bottle filling ~90% of the frame height, 2000 × 2000 px.
2. Each product, front, on the **warm backdrop**, same framing.
3. Each product, a label close-up (front panel), and one of the Supplement Facts side.
4. Group shots: the 3 sleep/calm/focus bottles together; all 6 together (wide 16:9 and square).
5. 4–6 lifestyle shots: bottle on a nightstand, kitchen counter, gym bag, desk (no people needed).

**Delivery:** full-resolution JPG + PNG, sRGB; white-background files clipped clean.

## Job post (copy into Upwork)

**Title:** Product photographer — supplement bottle packshots (white + warm studio) + lifestyle

**Description:**
New premium supplement brand. Our supplier ships you 7 products (5 white bottles with dark matte labels + 2 powder tubs); you keep them after the shoot. US address needed. On arrival we need quick phone photos so we can check the label print before the shoot.

We need: clean e-commerce packshots on pure white (Google Shopping ready), the same on a warm cream studio backdrop,
label close-ups, two group shots, and 4–6 simple lifestyle scenes (no models). Full shot list on hire. Commercial
usage rights for web, ads and social are required.

Please share 3 packshot sets you've shot (supplements, skincare or similar), your price for the full list including
2 retouching rounds, and turnaround.
