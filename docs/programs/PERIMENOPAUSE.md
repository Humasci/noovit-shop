# Perimenopause program: plan (23 Nov 2026)

**Goal.** A free, evidence-led program people get in exchange for their email. It builds the list for the Women's Health range,
the Perimenopause Check (at-home test) and Menopause Balance+. It's also strong SEO and social content: "perimenopause" and
"perimenopause symptoms" are among the biggest women's-health searches (docs/seo/ahrefs).

**Working name:** "Perimenopause, Decoded: a 6-week guide". Alternatives: "The Perimenopause Playbook", "Know Your Change".

## What people get
1. **Landing page** `/programs/perimenopause`.
   - **Hero:** an animated illustration of how estrogen and progesterone rise and fall more unevenly in perimenopause, drawn as smooth bead lines in our style and labelled "illustrative".
   - **Then:** what you'll learn each week, who it's for, and an FAQ.
   - **Sign-up:** email only.
   - Room for short videos later (anatomy-visual style, `design/briefs/ANATOMY_VISUALS.md`).
2. **Instant download (PDF, printable)** "Perimenopause starter kit":
   - a 4-week symptom tracker (sleep, hot flashes, mood, energy, cycle)
   - "questions to ask your doctor", including HRT as a doctor conversation
   - an evidence-graded table of what helps
   - when to see a doctor (red flags)
3. **Six weekly emails** (automation from sign-up), plain and short, each ending with one small action:
   1. **Welcome + the kit:** what perimenopause is (it can start in your 40s, it lasts years, and symptoms vary).
   2. **Your hormones, simply:** why cycles change, and why a single blood test is only a snapshot.
   3. **Sleep:** night sweats, sleep habits, CBT-I. Magnesium evidence, graded.
   4. **Hot flashes:** triggers, cooling, CBT. What the evidence says about black cohosh and others (mixed), and what to discuss with a doctor (hormonal and non-hormonal options).
   5. **Strength, bones and energy:** resistance training, protein, vitamin D. Creatine (evidence in women, graded).
   6. **Mood, brain fog and talking to your doctor:** what to bring (your tracker); the Perimenopause Check, coming soon.

## Evidence and compliance
- **Sources:** The Menopause Society (2022 hormone therapy and 2023 non-hormonal therapy position statements), ACOG, NIH (NIA, NCCIH, ODS) and peer-reviewed reviews.
- **Same rules as our guides:** every claim sourced and graded (Moderate / Limited / Early / Insufficient), null results included.
- **Education first:** no product mention in the PDF. A soft "if you want to try…" with our products appears only in emails 3 to 6, with structure/function wording and the disclaimer. HRT is presented neutrally as a doctor decision.
- **Privacy:** email only. We don't store tracker answers or symptoms (that would be consumer health data). Stored as a subscriber with source `program-perimenopause`; no analytics event names the program topic beyond "lead".
- **Medical review:** ideally the dietitian reviews before launch (same pattern as guides: "Reviewed by" only after a real review).
