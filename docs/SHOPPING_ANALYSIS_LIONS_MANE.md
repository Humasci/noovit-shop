### Formula check: much better than berberine, with two caveats
1. **The dose is fair.** 1,000 mg a day matches the two direct competitors (Cortex and Brainstorm, both 1,000 mg). Human studies used more: **3 g/day** of fruiting body powder (Mori 2009) and **3.2 g/day** (Saitsu 2019). So we're at the low end of the evidence, like most of the market.
2. **"Fruiting body + mycelium" and "40% polysaccharides" are the weak spots.**
   - Mycelium is usually grown on grain, and "polysaccharides" counts that grain starch. **Beta-glucans** are the meaningful measure (good fruiting-body products show ≥25–30%).
   - Mushroom-savvy buyers and reviewers know this, and our current catalog entry says "fruiting body, 30% beta-glucans", which doesn't match.
   - **Ask Supliful for a COA with beta-glucan %** (and whether alpha-glucan/starch is tested). If beta-glucans are low, word the label honestly ("fruiting body & mycelium") and don't make beta-glucan claims.

- Our **option B (clinical white)** would stand out as the only clean white label among dark ones. **Option D (amber + cream label)** would sit right next to Innerbody Labs, the brand you liked.
