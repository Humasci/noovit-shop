# Perimenopause, Decoded: photo brief for Gemini (23 Nov 2026)

These photos are for the free program page `/programs/perimenopause` and its emails. They replace the two stock photos used
for now.

**Rules:**
- **No products, packaging, logos or text in any image:** the program is education first.
- **Look:**
  - Real-looking women aged 42 to 55, varied ethnicities.
  - Natural skin and hair, gray strands welcome, light retouching only.
  - Calm, warm, confident; not sad, not "suffering".
  - Soft daylight; the palette leans to the page colors: lilac #E8E2F2, sage #D9E0D2, cream #EFE9DF and white.
- **Format:** 4:5 portrait, 1600 × 2000 (crops to 1:1 for email), except photo 2, which is 16:9 landscape (2000 × 1125).

## The photos
1. **"Is this for me?" (replaces the woman drinking water):** woman, about 47, at a sunny kitchen table in a soft lilac or cream knit,
   writing in a small notebook (her symptom tracker), a cup of tea beside her, relaxed half-smile. Background softly out of focus,
   warm cream walls.
2. **"Evidence, not hype" (replaces the dancing group), landscape:** three or four women in their 40s and 50s walking and laughing
   together on a path in a green park in late-afternoon light, in light clothes (sage, cream, white). Natural, candid, not posed.
3. **Week 3, sleep (for the email and a future section):** woman, about 50, in a bright, calm bedroom at dusk, sitting on the bed edge
   in light loungewear, stretching her arms and shoulders, eyes closed. Lilac and white bedding, warm lamp.
4. **Week 5, strength:** woman, about 52, at home or in a bright studio, doing a goblet squat with a light kettlebell, focused
   and strong, in sage athletic wear. White or cream background.
5. **Week 6, the doctor's appointment:** woman, about 48, sitting across from a female doctor in a bright clinic, showing her
   notebook; the doctor is listening and leaning in. Friendly, equal, not clinical-cold. (No visible charts, screens or text.)

**Prompt pattern:** "Photorealistic lifestyle photograph, natural soft daylight, [scene]. [Woman, age, ethnicity, clothing in
lilac/sage/cream]. Calm, warm, premium health-brand look. No text, no logos, no products or packaging. Realistic hands and
faces. [4:5 vertical | 16:9 landscape]."

**Delivery:** send the results as file attachments.
